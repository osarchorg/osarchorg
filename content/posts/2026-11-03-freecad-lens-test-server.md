The FreeCAD Project Association has [announced a Lens test server](https://blog.freecad.org/2026/09/18/announcing-lens-test-server/) at [lens.freecad.org](https://lens.freecad.org/) for collaborating on FreeCAD projects. Based on Ondsel Lens, the product data management platform supports organisations and team members, project versioning, and model sharing with optional password protection. A [FreeCAD add-on](https://github.com/FreeCAD/Ondsel-Lens-Addon/) connects the desktop application to the server.

Development has continued after Ondsel closed in late 2024, supported by a five-figure donation through the Ondsel Onwards Fund. The announcement highlights additions including on-premises deployment, a TrueNAS app, OAuth and OIDC authentication, and running custom Python scripts on models in the browser.

The FPA is inviting users to test the service and report problems or feature requests. [Documentation](https://freecad.github.io/lens-docs/) covers the platform, while the [server repository](https://github.com/FreeCAD/Ondsel-Server) and add-on repository provide separate issue trackers for contributing feedback.
