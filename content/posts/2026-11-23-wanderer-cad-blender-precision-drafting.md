[Wanderer CAD](https://github.com/Impressivetravel/wanderer-cad) is a free software add-on that adds precision drawing and modelling tools to Blender, with a workflow modelled on CAD software such as AutoCAD and Rhino. Published under GPL-3.0-or-later in November 2026, it is available from [Blender Extensions](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/wanderer/), where it can be installed and updated from inside Blender.

Commands are typed into a command line at the bottom of the viewport, with aliases and typed lengths, angles and coordinates. Drawing is supported by object snaps, smart and polar tracking, Ortho mode, grid snap and axis locks. Tools cover curves such as polylines, arcs and circles, curve editing including trim, fillet, offset and extend, and surfaces and solids including extrude, loft, sweep, booleans and PushPull.

For drafting, the add-on provides dimensions, hatches, blocks and layers built on Blender collections. Layouts place viewports of the model at scale on paper sheets with a title block, and export to PDF, SVG and DXF; DXF drawings can also be imported with their layers, colours, blocks and text. The add-on is developed by a designer who describes it as the tool missing from their own work, and the repository includes a reference guide and accepts bug reports.
