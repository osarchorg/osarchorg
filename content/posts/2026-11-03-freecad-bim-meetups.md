FreeCAD has [announced weekly BIM meetups](https://blog.freecad.org/2026/10/09/announcing-freecad-bim-meetups/) where developers and active users discuss workbench development, future plans and the roadmap. Initiated by community member marcuspollio, the meetings provide a regular forum for coordinating contributions as the BIM development team grows.

The September sessions addressed preparations for the FreeCAD 26.3 feature freeze and shared goals for 2D drawing creation, floor-plan modelling, and improvements to the door and window tool. A [public document](https://cloud.freecad.org/s/Ndz4teRNANWztMy) contains meeting notes and the rolling agenda, allowing contributors to follow discussions and ongoing work.

The announcement lists meetings on **Tuesdays at 16:00 UTC**, held online through Jitsi. Anyone interested in the BIM workbench can participate or listen. Check the [FreeCAD events calendar](https://www.freecad.org/events.php) for the joining link and current schedule.
