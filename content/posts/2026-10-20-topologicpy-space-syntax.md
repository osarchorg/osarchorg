Wassim Jabi has outlined [new space syntax capabilities in TopologicPy](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/space-syntax-python-new-capabilities-topologicpy-wassim-jabi-qgfxc/), bringing analyses of visibility and spatial accessibility into the free software library's Python geometry and graph workflow. Users can build visibility graphs from floor plans, calculate connectivity, global and local integration, and mean depth, then colour floor cells with the results.

The tools also cover approximate axial maps, segment analysis, angular integration and Choice, plus closeness and betweenness centrality. Angular, metric and topological radii allow analysis at different scales. Normalised angular measures and segment-length weighting are available, while isovist tools describe the area visible from a point using measures including area, perimeter and compactness.

Combining geometry and analysis in Python lets architects generate designs, attach results to geometry and compare alternatives within a notebook or application, alongside other data analysis and optimisation tools. Jabi credits DepthmapX as the foundation for this work and notes that TopologicPy covers selected analyses, without claiming complete feature parity.
